The 2024 Toyota Hilux is the latest iteration of Toyota's iconic pickup truck, known for its durability, reliability, and off-road capabilities. Whether you're tackling tough terrains or cruising through the city, the Hilux is built to deliver.

Key Features

Design and Exterior

Updated styling with an aggressive front grille and LED headlights. Rugged body-on-frame construction. Available in various trims with 17-inch or 18-inch alloy wheels.

Engine and Performance

2.8L Turbo Diesel: 201 HP, 369 lb-ft torque. 2.7L Petrol Engine: 164 HP, 181 lb-ft torque. 6-speed manual or automatic transmission. Towing capacity up to 3,500 kg (7,716 lbs).

Interior and Technology

Comfortable cabin with high-quality materials. 8-inch touchscreen with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. Toyota Safety Sense suite (adaptive cruise control, lane departure warning, etc.).

Off-Road Capabilities

Enhanced suspension for better off-road performance. Multiple drive modes (Eco, Normal, Power). Rear differential lock for improved traction.

Fuel Efficiency

The 2024 Hilux offers excellent fuel efficiency, with the 2.8L diesel engine achieving an estimated 7-9 L/100 km (33-26 mpg).

Trim Levels

Workmate: Base model for utility-focused buyers.
SR: Mid-range trim with added features.
SR5: Top-tier trim with premium features.
Rogue: Luxurious variant with additional comfort and tech (select markets).
