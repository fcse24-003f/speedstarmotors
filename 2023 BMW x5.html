The 2023 BMW X5 is a premium SUV that combines luxury, performance, and advanced technology. Whether you're navigating city streets or exploring off-road trails, the X5 is designed to impress.

Key Features

Design and Exterior

Elegant and sporty design with a bold kidney grille and LED headlights. Sleek body lines and available in various trims with 19-inch or 21-inch alloy wheels.

Engine and Performance

3.0L Turbo Inline-6: 335 HP, 330 lb-ft torque. 4.4L Twin-Turbo V8: 523 HP, 553 lb-ft torque. 8-speed automatic transmission. All-wheel drive (xDrive) available.

Interior and Technology

Luxurious cabin with premium materials. 12.3-inch touchscreen with BMW iDrive 7.0, Apple CarPlay, and Android Auto. Advanced safety features including adaptive cruise control, lane departure warning, and more.

Off-Road Capabilities

Adaptive suspension for a smooth ride on various terrains. Multiple drive modes (Eco, Comfort, Sport). Hill descent control for enhanced off-road performance.

Fuel Efficiency

The 2023 X5 offers competitive fuel efficiency, with the 3.0L engine achieving an estimated 10-12 L/100 km (23-20 mpg).

Trim Levels

xDrive40i: Base model with robust features.
xDrive50i: Enhanced performance and luxury.
M50i: High-performance variant with sporty features.
Plug-in Hybrid: Eco-friendly option with electric range.
