The 2024 Ford Mustang is a legendary sports car that combines powerful performance, advanced technology, and striking design. Whether you're cruising on the highway or hitting the racetrack, the Mustang is built to impress.

Key Features

Design and Exterior

Aggressive and aerodynamic design with a bold front grille and LED headlights. Sleek body lines and available in various trims with 18-inch or 19-inch alloy wheels.

Engine and Performance

2.3L EcoBoost: 310 HP, 350 lb-ft torque. 5.0L V8: 450 HP, 410 lb-ft torque. 6-speed manual or 10-speed automatic transmission. Rear-wheel drive.

Interior and Technology

Sporty cabin with premium materials. 12-inch digital instrument cluster, SYNC 4 infotainment system with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. Advanced safety features including adaptive cruise control, lane-keeping assist, and more.

Performance Features

Enhanced suspension for superior handling. Multiple drive modes (Normal, Sport, Track). Limited-slip differential for improved traction.

Fuel Efficiency

The 2024 Mustang offers competitive fuel efficiency, with the 2.3L EcoBoost engine achieving an estimated 9-11 L/100 km (26-21 mpg).

Trim Levels

EcoBoost: Base model with robust features.
GT: Enhanced performance and luxury.
Mach 1: High-performance variant with sporty features.
Shelby GT500: Ultimate performance with track-ready capabilities.
